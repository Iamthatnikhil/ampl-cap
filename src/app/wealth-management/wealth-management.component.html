One Platform, Every Financial Decision

We bring your loans, investments and insurance together under a single, unified view, so your money moves with a plan, not just a transaction.

One size certainly doesn't fit all, so why should your financial planning?

At AMPL Wealth, we build a personalised financial roadmap for every client, connecting borrowing, investing and protection into one coherent strategy, backed by tools you can track from the app.

Our simple 3-step approach:

  • Understanding your goals and risk appetite
  • Structuring a plan across loans, investments and insurance
  • Continuous tracking and rebalancing through the AMPL Wealth app

Our Wealth Management Services

  • Loans

    Personal, home, vehicle and business loans, sized against your broader financial plan rather than approved in isolation.
    We look at your existing investments, cover and liabilities before recommending how much and how you should borrow, so debt supports your plan instead of working against it.

  • Investments

    Mutual funds, SIPs and long-term instruments chosen against a defined goal and time frame, not a one-off product sale.
    Whether you're investing for a home, your child's education or retirement, we help you pick a mix that matches your time horizon and risk appetite.

  • Insurance

    Term, health and motor cover sized to match your dependents, assets and existing liabilities.
    We review your cover alongside your investments and loans, so protection keeps pace as your income, family and assets grow.

Why AMPL Wealth

  • One unified view of your loans, investments and insurance, instead of separate apps and statements to piece together yourself.

  • Goal-based planning that works backward from what you're saving for — a home, an education, a retirement date — rather than a generic product pitch.

  • Simple calculators for SIPs, EMIs and cover, so you can estimate outcomes before you commit to any product.

  • Ongoing tracking through the AMPL Wealth app, with regular reviews so your plan adjusts as your life does.

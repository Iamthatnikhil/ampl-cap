About Ampl Capital (formerly Credent Global Finance Ltd.)

Ampl was founded with a simple idea “Financing solutions don’t have to be confusing”.

Back in 2017, when we started, there were NBFCs, real estate advisors, investment banking firms, wealth management firms, but there wasn’t a single firm that could do it all and that could do it well. Did that mean the investor had no choice but to entrust 10 different businesses with their hard-earned money, with their portfolio, and with their and their loved ones’ future?

Can you even trust one these days!

This is where Ampl comes in.
Our panel of experts has been crafting practical executable strategies since 2017 for everything – from equity to raising funds for your business, from acquisitions & mergers to disquisitions, from savings to real estate purchase, from India to the UK.
